# November 19, 2025 : The Dragon's Trial
Provide an overall recap, describing why they’re here and the larger picture, based on their understanding 

A long journey it has been. From the edges of the [[Sun Striped Plains\|Sun Striped Plains]], now on the other side of [[The Spine\|The Spine]]. Encountering many natural creatures wreaking havoc, as well as a cultist group known as [[Legion\|Legion]], seeming to attempt a way to find [[Amon, Primal of Death\|The Demon Lord, Amon]], wanting to "be on the right side" when the inevitable events unfold.

First encountering them in [[Theltgalor\|Theltgalor]], where the cultist group has stolen an item, yet to be known to the party, and had performed a ritual in the depths of an ancient, abandoned temple of Amon, where the party was moments behind them finishing the ritual. The results...the party may never know.

Heading to a potential looting area, tipped off by [[Garrick\|Garrick]] in [[Beckton\|Beckton]] that there may be something in a cave on the other side of [[The Spine\|The Spine]]. Along their travels, the party discovered there was indeed something worth while in this place, where they overheard that another group had inquired about passage through the [[Thaldrim Pass\|Thaldrim Pass]]. With the use of clever magic and well executed plans, the party was able to close in on the other party before they left the cave. Stumbling in on a handful of [[Legion\|Legion]] Cultists attempting their leave. 

Besting the group, you found their quest took them to this cave for a map, marked with several Xs across all of Agiezall. Touching the map, Beedle was blessed with sight, the most basic understanding of [[World & Lore/General Lore/Vox Umbrarum - Noctisong\|Vox Umbrarum - Noctisong]], granting him some powers of darkness.

Also in the cave, a set of stone armor called out to Rokirus. An armor granted by the Gods themselves to allow the mortals better fighting chances against the remnant. 

Loot was not all Fate had in store. Inserting 2 crystals found along their journey, a portal appeared in the sone wall. What we now understand to be a trial of a sort. The reward, a dragon companion. 

The party currently finds themselves fighting against 3 angelic warriors, Beedle, lay on the ground unconscious from the first fatal blow of one of the celestial beings.
